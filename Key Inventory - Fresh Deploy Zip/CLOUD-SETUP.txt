BAND SYNC - FIREBASE SETUP GUIDE
================================

What your band gets
-------------------
A new person only has to REGISTER WITH THEIR EMAIL (and a password). Then they search for a friend by
email and tap "Add friend". The friend sees a card and taps Accept, and from then on you are band
members together and your songs and setlists stay in sync.

Everyone except the person who hosts the app does NOTHING else: no settings to paste, no rules, no codes.
The host does the steps below ONE TIME (about 10 minutes). It uses Firebase, Google's free service (the
"Spark" plan is plenty for a church band).

HOST SETUP (one time)
---------------------
1. Go to https://console.firebase.google.com, sign in with Google, click "Create a project", name it
   (for example "Key Inventory"). You can turn Google Analytics off.

2. Turn on sign-in:  Authentication > Get started > Sign-in method > Email/Password > switch "Enable"
   on > Save.

3. Make the database:  Firestore Database (it may be under "Databases & Storage") > Create database >
   keep "Production mode" > pick a location near you > Create.

4. Publish the rules. Open the file  firestore.rules  from this folder (Notepad is fine), press Ctrl+A
   then Ctrl+C. In Firebase go to Firestore Database > Rules tab, delete what is there, press Ctrl+V,
   and click "Publish". (The same rules cover everything the app does: songs, setlists, members, who is
   playing, play invitations and chat. You do not need to change them for new versions of the app.)

5. Get your project's settings:  click Settings (gear, left menu) > General > scroll to "Your apps" >
   click the web icon </> > give it any nickname > Register app. Firebase shows a block like:

       const firebaseConfig = {
         apiKey: "AIza...",
         authDomain: "your-project.firebaseapp.com",
         projectId: "your-project",
         storageBucket: "your-project.firebasestorage.app",
         messagingSenderId: "1234567890",
         appId: "1:1234567890:web:abcdef"
       };

6. Open  firebase.js  from this folder (Notepad). Replace each PASTE_... value with the matching value
   from the block (keep the quote marks). Save.

7. Upload ALL the files of this folder (index.html, firebase.js, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest and the three
   icon-*.png files) TOGETHER to your website (Netlify, GitHub Pages, ...). Open the website address
   (https://...). Done: anybody who opens it can register.

OPTIONAL: Firebase Hosting (no firebase.js to fill in at all)
-------------------------------------------------------------
If you host the app on Firebase Hosting, the app finds your project's settings by itself (steps 5 and 6
are not needed). With the Firebase tools installed, run "firebase deploy" in this folder: it publishes the
website AND the rules (firebase.json and firestore.rules are included for that).

TEST IT WITH TWO PHONES
-----------------------
1. Phone A: open the address, Settings > Band & sync, register with an email and a password.
2. Phone B: the same, with another email.
3. Phone A: Settings > Band & sync > search for B's email > Add friend.
4. Phone B: a card appears: tap Accept.
5. Add a song on A: it appears on B within a few seconds (after A taps the check button).
6. On A open a setlist and tap Play; B shows a short "is playing" notice and a yellow Join button.

IF SOMETHING DOES NOT WORK
--------------------------
- The app says sync is "not switched on yet": firebase.js still has its PASTE_ words, or it was not
  uploaded next to index.html. Fill it in, upload again, then reload the page.
- Works in Safari but not from the iPhone Home Screen: the Home Screen app keeps its OWN saved data. If you
  once pasted your Firebase settings inside the app, they are saved only for Safari. Put them in
  firebase.js (Settings > Band & sync > "Save firebase.js" in Safari makes the file for you), upload it
  next to index.html, remove the Home Screen app, reload the site in Safari and add it again. Then sign in
  once inside the Home Screen app.
- "Check connection" (Settings > Band & sync) tests each step and says which one fails.
- You see old behaviour after an update: phones keep the old page. Reload it, or remove the Home Screen
  app and add it again.
- A person keeps showing as playing: Settings > Band & sync > Live play shows who the app thinks is
  playing. For your own other device there is a "Remove it" button. In the Firebase console you can also
  delete the document in workspaces > (your band) > live.
- Phone alerts do nothing: they need sw.js, manifest.webmanifest and the icon files uploaded next to
  index.html, and "Turn on" in Settings > Notifications. On iPhone the app must be added to the Home
  Screen first. If the app is fully closed, alerts wait until it is opened again (alerts to a fully
  closed phone need a push server, which is not included).
- Sign-in says the email is already in use: that person already has an account, so they should sign in.
  Passwords are reset in Firebase console > Authentication > Users.

DELETING DATA
-------------
Back up first (Settings > Your data > Backup). Firebase console > Authentication > Users deletes sign-in
accounts. Firestore Database > Data > three-dot menu > Delete collection deletes data: users, emails and
workspaces hold the accounts' details and the band's songs, setlists, members, play records and chat. Deleting
an account does not delete its data, and deleting the band's data also removes those songs from members'
devices when they next sync. Nothing can be undone.
